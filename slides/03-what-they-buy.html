▸ What They're Really Buying
Clients don't buy gold.
They buy certainty.
Every client who picks up the phone is carrying a question
they won't say out loud:

Can I trust this person with what I have?

They're not evaluating your product.
They're evaluating your judgment.
Give them that — and the asset almost sells itself.

Editorial Philosophy
"

Stop asking whether they want to buy gold. Start asking what percentage of their portfolio they want working against them in a negative real yield environment. The client who frames it as "should I buy gold?" is making an emotional decision. The client who frames it as "how much of my wealth do I want in an asset mathematically guaranteed to lose purchasing power?" is making a mathematical one. Guide them to the second framing. The decision usually resolves itself.
